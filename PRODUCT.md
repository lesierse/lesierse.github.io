# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Two equally important client groups:
- Small and mid-sized businesses and startups that need an application designed and built end to end.
- Enterprises that need senior consultancy or contracting on software architecture, cloud and AI.

Both arrive wanting to judge, quickly, whether this one-person studio has the depth to be trusted with their work, and then to get in touch.

## Product Purpose
Lesierse IT is a newly started independent IT business run by Vincent Lesierse. It delivers application development and consultancy for software development, cloud and AI. The website exists to explain the offer, show credible depth, and turn visitors into an email conversation.

## Positioning
A single senior engineer-architect who both advises and builds, and who ships his own apps (such as Ace31), so advice comes from someone who still ships and operates real software.

## Operating Context
Visitors are decision makers and technical leads evaluating a supplier, typically on desktop during work hours, sometimes on mobile from a LinkedIn link. Dutch and international audiences.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: application development (web, mobile, back-end), cloud architecture and migration (AWS and Azure), AI integration, software/architecture consultancy.
- Skills to share (without naming employers): 20+ years of software engineering; .NET and full-stack development; cloud-native architecture; containers and Kubernetes; infrastructure as code (CDK, Terraform); DevOps and CI/CD; modernising legacy systems into cloud-native/microservice SaaS; multi-tenant SaaS; open-source contribution and conference speaking.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at lesierse.it, plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.
- Bilingual: English and Dutch.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Lesierse IT.
- Tone: professional, simple, direct.
- Do not name past employers (e.g. AWS, Microsoft); express experience as skills.

## Product Principles
1. Show depth through specifics, not superlatives.
2. One clear action: start a conversation by email.
3. Builder and advisor in one — keep both visible.
4. Honest and small: a one-person studio, not a pretend agency.
